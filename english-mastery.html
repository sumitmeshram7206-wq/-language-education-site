The Four Level Pathway

Start where you are. Go further.

A short speaking, listening, reading and writing diagnostic helps identify your entry level.

NWE-101 · Level 1

English Foundation

40 hours recommended

For beginners and hesitant speakers.

  • Basic sentences and everyday vocabulary
  • Clear pronunciation and simple conversations
  • Simple reading and writing
Ask about NWE-101 →

NWE-201 · Level 2

English Communication

50 hours recommended

For learners with basic English who need fluency.

  • Longer conversations and explaining opinions
  • Listening practice and group discussion
  • Speaking with greater clarity and confidence
Ask about NWE-201 →

NWE-301 · Level 3

Professional English

60 hours recommended

For graduates and working professionals.

  • Professional emails and meeting communication
  • Presentations and workplace speaking
  • Resumes and interview communication
Ask about NWE-301 →

NWE-401 · Level 4

Advanced English &
Global Communication

60 hours recommended

For independent speakers seeking advanced skills.

  • Persuasive speaking and nuanced writing
  • Debates, negotiation and presentations
  • Cross-cultural communication
